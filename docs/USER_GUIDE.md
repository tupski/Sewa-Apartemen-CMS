# User Guide — Sewa Apartemen CMS

## Browsing Properties

Visit the homepage to see featured properties. Each property card shows the name, location, and status. Click a property to view its units and details.

## Finding a Unit to Rent

Each property page lists available units with:
- Unit type (Studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR)
- Floor number and size (m²)
- Bedroom / bathroom counts
- Monthly or nightly price

Use the filter on the units page to narrow by type or price range.

## Booking a Unit

1. On a unit's detail page, click **"Book Now"** or navigate to `/units/{slug}/booking`.
2. Fill in the booking form:
   - **Full name** (required)
   - **Phone number** (required, WhatsApp preferred)
   - **Email** (optional)
   - **Check-in** and **Check-out** dates
   - **Number of guests**
   - Optional message
3. Click **Submit Booking**.
4. You'll see a confirmation page with your unique **booking code**. Keep this code for reference.
5. The administrator will review your booking and contact you via WhatsApp/phone to confirm.

## Reading the Blog

Visit `/blog` to browse articles. Filter by category or tag using the sidebar. Click any post title to read the full article.

## Contact

The contact information is displayed in the site footer. Look for the WhatsApp number, email address, and social media links configured by the administrator.

## Custom Pages

The site may include custom pages like "About Us", "Terms & Conditions", or "Privacy Policy". These are accessible via the main navigation menu.
